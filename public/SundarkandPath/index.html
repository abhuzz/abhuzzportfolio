A peaceful companion for your Path

Everything for a calm, complete Sundarkand

Every chaupai, doha, soratha and chhand, from "Shri Ganeshay Namah" to the final doha.

📖

Every verse, with meaning

Hindi or English bhavarth under each line. Keep the original Devanagari visible if you like.

🌏

15 scripts

The recitation stays in Tulsidas's original Awadhi, and the text follows in the script you read best.

🎧

Follow any recitation

Play your favourite Sundarkand on YouTube, Spotify or any music app. Tap the line being sung, and the text keeps pace with that singer.

🔊

Five ways to read

Speaking + auto scroll, silent auto scroll, line by line, just reading, or follow external audio.

🪔

Prepare with devotion

A gentle Panchtattva checklist: Snan, clean clothes, Asan, Deep-Dhoop-Pushp, a silent space, and Dhyan & Sankalp.

🌅

Wake for Brahma Muhurta

Reminders on Tuesday and Saturday, Hanumanji's days, at 4:00 AM, or daily at your chosen time.

🧘

A quiet, focused space

Meditation mode keeps the screen awake and can run your iPhone Focus. A child lock keeps the Path uninterrupted.

✨

Hanumanji's glow

Golden sparks gently gather into Hanumanji's face behind the verses as you read.
