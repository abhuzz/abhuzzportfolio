The opening of Sundarkand

चौपाई

जामवंत के बचन सुहाए।
सुनि हनुमंत हृदय अति भाए॥

Hearing Jambavan's beautiful words, Hanumanji's heart was filled with joy, and so the Sundarkand begins.

A peaceful companion for your Path

Everything for a calm, complete Sundarkand

Every chaupai, doha, soratha and chhand, from "Shri Ganeshay Namah" to the final doha, then Shri Hanuman Chalisa and Shri Hanumanji ki Aarti.

Every verse, with meaning

Hindi or English bhavarth under each line, with the original Devanagari kept visible if you like.

15 scripts

The recitation stays in Tulsidas's original Awadhi, and the text follows in the script you read best.

Follow any recitation

Play Sundarkand on YouTube, Spotify or any music app. Tap the line being sung, and the text keeps pace with your singer.

Five ways to read

A reciting voice, silent auto scroll, line by line, just reading, or following external audio.

Prepare with devotion

A gentle Panchtattva checklist: Snan, clean clothes, Asan, Deep-Dhoop-Pushp, silence, and Dhyan & Sankalp.

Wake for Brahma Muhurta

Reminders on Tuesday and Saturday, Hanumanji's days, at 4:00 AM, or daily at your chosen time.

A quiet, focused space

Meditation mode keeps the screen awake and can run your iPhone Focus. A child lock keeps the Path uninterrupted.

Hanumanji's glow

Golden sparks gently gather into Hanumanji's face behind the verses as you read.

Inside the app

Made for devotion

Calm colours, clear Devanagari and a gentle rhythm, from preparation to the final doha.

Read in your own script

15 languages, one Sundarkand

Switch at any time, even in the middle of the Path. Your place is always kept.
