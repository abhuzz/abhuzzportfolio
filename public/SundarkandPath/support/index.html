2Frequently asked questions

Reading

How do I change the reading script or language?

Open Settings → Language, or tap the 文A button at the top of the Path screen. You can switch at any time, even in the middle of the Path, and your place is kept.

How do I show or hide the meaning (bhavarth)?

In Settings → Meaning (भावार्थ), choose Off, हिन्दी or English. If you read in a script other than Devanagari, you can also turn on Show original Devanagari to see the original text beneath your script.

Why does Tamil say "approximate"?

The Tamil script has no separate letters for some sounds in the original Awadhi, such as aspirated and voiced consonants. The Tamil text is a close transliteration, so please follow the recitation for exact pronunciation.

Will the app remember where I stopped?

Yes. The home screen shows a Continue from verse … button that takes you back to the exact line.

How do I make the text bigger or the recitation slower?

Use Settings → Text size and Recitation pace. You can also adjust them from the controls at the bottom of the Path screen.

Listening & following along

What are the five reading modes?
  • Speaking + auto scroll: a voice recites, and each line glows as it is read.
  • Mute + auto scroll: silent, and the lines move at your reading pace.
  • Mute + manual scroll: you move line by line yourself.
  • Just reading: scroll freely, like a book.
  • Follow external audio: the text follows a recording you play in another app.

Change the mode from the button above the play controls on the Path screen, or in Settings → How the Path moves.

How do I follow a recording from YouTube or Spotify?
  • Choose Follow external audio, then open your Sundarkand recording (the app has quick links to YouTube and Spotify) and start it.
  • Come back to the app and press ▶ when the singer reaches the highlighted line.
  • Whenever the singer is ahead or behind, tap the line being sung. The app re-syncs and learns that singer's pace, so after two or three taps it keeps pace by itself.

iPhone doesn't let one app hear another app's audio, so syncing works through your taps.

I can't hear the voice in "Speaking" mode.
  • Check that your device isn't on silent and that the media volume is up.
  • Check Settings → Voice volume in the app.
  • The voice uses your device's built-in speech voices. If it still sounds missing, restart the app.

Reminders, Focus & child lock

My reminders don't arrive.
  • Allow notifications in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Sundarkand Path.
  • In the app, check that the Tuesday & Saturday reminder, or the daily reminder, is switched on and the time is correct.
  • Focus modes and Low Power Mode can delay or hide notifications.
How does Meditation mode silence my phone?

Meditation mode keeps the screen awake during the Path. To also pause calls and messages, follow Settings → Set up iPhone Focus once. It guides you through creating a "Sundarkand Focus" shortcut, which the app runs when your Path begins.

How do I unlock the child lock?

While the Path is playing with Child lock on, the screen ignores touches. Press and hold the lock button for 2 seconds to unlock. You can turn Child lock on or off in Settings.

Why does iPhone show "You have changed the icon" sometimes?

That message comes from iOS when the Day & night icon switches between the morning and moonlit icons. To stop it, turn off Settings → App icon → Day & night icon.

Privacy & account

Do I need an account? Is my data collected?

No account is needed, and we don't collect, track or sell any personal data. Your settings and reading progress stay on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I reset everything?

Deleting the app removes all its settings and progress from your device. When you reinstall it, the app starts fresh.

3Report a correction

We take great care with every verse and meaning. If you spot a mistake in the text, a transliteration or a meaning, please email us with the verse number (shown at the bottom of the Path screen, for example "48 / 669"), the script you were reading, and the correction. 🙏
