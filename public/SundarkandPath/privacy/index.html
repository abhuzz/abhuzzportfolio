1Who we are

Sundarkand Path ("the app", "we", "us") is a devotional app for reading Shri Sundarkand from Goswami Tulsidas's Shri Ramcharitmanas. It is created and maintained by Abhay Korat, an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles information on iPhone, iPad and other supported devices.

2Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and it does not create an account. It contains:

  • no analytics or usage tracking,
  • no advertising or ad identifiers,
  • no third-party software that collects data,
  • no servers of ours that the app connects to.

The Sundarkand text, meanings and recitation audio are bundled inside the app, so the app works fully offline.

3Information stored on your device

To work as you expect, the app saves a few preferences locally on your device only:

  • your reading script, meaning language, text size, recitation pace and voice volume,
  • your reading mode and the verse where you stopped,
  • your reminder settings, and options such as child lock, meditation mode and the day-and-night icon,
  • your preparation checklist and the number of completed Paths.

This information never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see it. Your device's own backup, such as iCloud Backup, may include it according to your device settings. Deleting the app removes it.

4Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications (optional): to remind you of your Path, for example on Tuesday and Saturday at Brahma Muhurta. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Keeping the screen awake: so the screen doesn't turn off during a Path.
  • Speech: the "Speaking" mode uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices to recite the text on your device.
  • Time zone: read from your device so reminders arrive at the right local time. It is used only on your device.

5Links to other apps and services

Some buttons open other apps or websites, but only when you tap them:

  • YouTube or Spotify, to find a Sundarkand recording to follow,
  • Instagram, to visit @ab.korat,
  • your email app, to write to us,
  • the iOS Shortcuts app, to run the optional "Sundarkand Focus" shortcut you set up yourself.

Once you leave our app, the other service's own privacy policy applies. We don't receive any information from those services.

6When you contact us

If you choose to send feedback or a request, from the app's Connect & About screen or directly, it is sent through your own email app. We will then receive what you choose to include, such as your email address, name and message. We use it only to read and reply to you, and to improve the app. We don't share it with anyone, add you to mailing lists, or use it for marketing. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

7Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The optional child lock helps keep a Path uninterrupted when little ones are nearby.

8Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You stay in control on your device:

  • change or turn off notifications in your device settings,
  • change any preference inside the app,
  • delete the app to remove everything it stored.

If you have emailed us and want those messages deleted, just ask. Depending on where you live, laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California may give you further rights. We are happy to help with any request.

9Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, for example if a future feature needs an internet connection, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If the change is significant, we will also explain it in the app's release notes.

10Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to abhaykorat@zohomail.in, or visit the Support page.
